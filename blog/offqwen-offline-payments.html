OFFQWEN: offline payments with zero signal

Money moves. Even with zero bars. OFFQWEN is payments for the places the network never reaches.

By Bishowdeep Bhusal — Vice CEO, co-founder & head developer, OFFQWEN

OFFQWEN is offline payments for Android. When there is no signal, a signed one-time note carries the payment by QR code, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi Direct or NFC — and it settles once someone, somewhere, gets signal again. The note is signed and one-time, so it cannot be spent twice.

In much of Nepal the network is the weak link. Payments should not stop when the bars disappear — and that is the whole point of OFFQWEN. It is the project behind the line on my homepage: payments with zero bars of signal, then I make them work.

I co-built OFFQWEN with Jitesh Prakash Chaudhary. The app is called Paila: its website, docs and releases are public, and the source code itself is private. It is test credit only — no real money — and you keep Play Protect on.
