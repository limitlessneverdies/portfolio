01Android · v0.3

NexPay

Money that moves without internet.

Half of Nepal knows the feeling: you're at a tea shop in the hills, you want to pay, and there's no signal. NexPay signs one-time payment notes on your phone and passes them along by QR, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi Direct or NFC. The person you pay can even pass it on before going online. It settles once you're back on the network, and it can only be spent once.

02Hackathon build · Gemini

Rakshak रक्षक

Make the job prove itself.

Lakhs of Nepalis leave for the Gulf every year. A lot of them get promised one thing on a voice note, then handed a contract that says something else, in a language they can't read. Rakshak takes the voice notes, chats, ads, contracts, receipts and visas and checks what was promised against what's actually on paper. You get one answer, read out loud in Nepali: STOP, CHECK, or MATCH.

03Reverse engineering · Rust

Alembic, opened up

I loved a chemistry game, so I took its engine apart.

Alembic is a falling-sand sandbox where you play with the periodic table. Learning chemistry in it is amazing, but there was no way to add anything new. So I went through the shipped Rust binary piece by piece: every element, every reaction, the save format, the shaders. Then I wrote a spec so the game can load mods. Once you can add stuff, there's no ceiling on what it can teach.

Focus Lock

Willpower isn't a strategy. Friction is.

You know the loop. Open the laptop to work, "just check" one tab, and suddenly three hours of YouTube are gone. So you sign a commitment, pick your days and pick your blocklist. After that, Alt+F4 doesn't work, Task Manager is gone, restarting just reopens it, and deleting it doesn't remove it. You chose this. Now you're stuck with it. The rebuild, MONOLITH, runs as a SYSTEM service, and the only way out early is a 5-stage cognitive test.
